it — this page is that permission, and it is deliberately short.

self-hostable repository and the hosted Sqemes Cloud service. We cannot ship your contribution in

reason for this document.

## How you sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment with the exact sentence to reply with. Replying to that
comment is the signature — there is no form, no account, and nothing to email.

You sign once. It covers everything you contribute afterwards.

If you would rather not sign, that is a legitimate position and we would still like to hear from you:
**open an issue instead.** A well-described problem is worth as much as a patch, and it carries no
licensing question at all.
